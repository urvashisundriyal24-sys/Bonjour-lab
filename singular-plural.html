🔢 Singular & Plural

Learn how French words change from one to many!

✨ One or More?

In French, singular means one person or thing, while plural means two or more.

📌 Basic Idea

👤 Singular

un livre

one book

📖 Le livre est intéressant.
The book is interesting.

👥 Plural

des livres

books

📚 Les livres sont intéressants.
The books are interesting.

👤 Singular

une maison

one house

🏠 La maison est grande.
The house is big.

👥 Plural

des maisons

houses

🏠🏠 Les maisons sont grandes.
The houses are big.

📚 Common Plural Rules

Singular Plural Meaning
un livre des livres book → books
une table des tables table → tables
un ami des amis friend → friends
une voiture des voitures car → cars
un étudiant des étudiants student → students

⭐ The Most Important Rule

💡 Usually, add “-s”

In many French nouns, the plural is formed by adding -s.

livre → livres
table → tables
voiture → voitures

⚠️ The final -s is usually not pronounced.
