🎯 Adjectives & Agreement

Learn how French adjectives change!

✨ What is an Adjective?

An adjective describes a person, place, animal or thing.

For example: grand = big / tall

In French, adjectives often change according to gender and number.

🧠 The Basic Rule

Masculine → Feminine

grand → grande

A common way to make an adjective feminine is to add -e.

🌸 Masculine & Feminine

👦 Grand / Grande

Un garçon grand.

A tall boy.

Une fille grande.
A tall girl.

😊 Petit / Petite

Un garçon petit.

A small boy.

Une fille petite.
A small girl.

💖 Content / Contente

Il est content.

He is happy.

Elle est contente.
She is happy.

😴 Fatigué / Fatiguée

Il est fatigué.

He is tired.

Elle est fatiguée.
She is tired.

🌈 Common A1 Adjectives

Masculine Feminine English
grand grande big / tall
petit petite small
content contente happy
fatigué fatiguée tired
intelligent intelligente intelligent
gentil gentille kind
sportif sportive sporty
beau belle beautiful / handsome

👥 Adjectives in the Plural

👨 Masculine Plural

Usually add -s.

grand → grands

Les garçons sont grands.

👩 Feminine Plural

Usually add -s.

grande → grandes

Les filles sont grandes.

💡 Easy Rule to Remember

Masculine singular: grand

Feminine singular: grande

Masculine plural: grands

Feminine plural: grandes

⭐ Always check whether the noun is masculine, feminine, singular or plural.

🧠 Memory Trick

👦 Masculine → grand
👧 Feminine → grande
👨‍👩‍👧 Plural → usually + S

Think: Gender + Number = Agreement 💡
