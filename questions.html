❓ Asking Questions

Learn how to ask simple questions in French!

✨ How Do We Ask Questions?

French has several easy ways to ask questions.

At A1 level, start with these three methods: intonation, est-ce que, and simple question words.

🗣️ 1. Intonation

💬 Easy Method

You can turn a statement into a question simply by raising your voice at the end.

Tu parles français.

You speak French.

Tu parles français ?
Do you speak French?

☕ Example

Tu aimes le café ?

Do you like coffee?

⭐ 2. Est-ce que

💡 A very useful question pattern

Put Est-ce que before the sentence.

Est-ce que + sentence ?

Example: Est-ce que tu parles français ?

Do you speak French?

🔍 3. Question Words

❓ Qui ?

Who?

Qui est-ce ?
Who is it?

❓ Où ?

Where?

Où habites-tu ?
Where do you live?

❓ Quand ?

When?

Quand arrives-tu ?
When are you arriving?

❓ Pourquoi ?

Why?

Pourquoi tu ris ?
Why are you laughing?

❓ Comment ?

How?

Comment ça va ?
How are you?

❓ Quoi ?

What?

Tu fais quoi ?
What are you doing?

📚 Question Word Cheat Sheet
