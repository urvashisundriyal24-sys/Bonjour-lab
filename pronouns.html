👤 Subject Pronouns

Les pronoms sujets — Learn who is doing the action!

🌟 Meet the French Pronouns!

A subject pronoun tells us who is doing an action.

Think of it as the little word that comes before the verb.

Je + parle = I speak

🧩 The 8 Essential Pronouns

🙋
Je
I
Je parle français.
I speak French.
👉
Tu
You — informal
Tu aimes le café.
You like coffee.
👨
Il
He
Il habite à Delhi.
He lives in Delhi.
👩
Elle
She
Elle est étudiante.
She is a student.
👭
Nous
We
Nous sommes amis.
We are friends.
👋
Vous
You — formal / plural
Vous êtes professeur.
You are a teacher.
👨‍👨‍👦
Ils
They — masculine / mixed
Ils parlent français.
They speak French.
👩‍👩‍👧
Elles
They — feminine
Elles sont étudiantes.
They are students.

🧠 Super Easy Memory Trick

Je I
Tu You
Il He
Elle She
Nous We
Vous You
Ils They
Elles They

💡 Important: “Vous” can mean one person formally or several people. “Ils” is used for a masculine group or a mixed group.
