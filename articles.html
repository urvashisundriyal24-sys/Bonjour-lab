📚 French Articles

Learn the small words that come before French nouns.

✨ What are Articles?

Articles are small words that come before nouns. In French, the article changes depending on gender and number.

un

Masculine • Singular

Used with one masculine noun.

un livre

une

Feminine • Singular

Used with one feminine noun.

une table

des

Plural • Indefinite

Used with several nouns.

des livres

le

Masculine • Definite

Used for a specific masculine noun.

le livre

la

Feminine • Definite

Used for a specific feminine noun.

la table

les

Plural • Definite

Used for specific plural nouns.

les livres

🧠 Quick Comparison

Article Type Example
un Masculine singular un livre
une Feminine singular une table
des Plural des livres
le Masculine definite le livre
la Feminine definite la table
les Plural definite les livres

💡 Easy Memory Trick

un → one masculine thing
une → one feminine thing
des → some / several things

le → the masculine noun
la → the feminine noun
les → the plural noun
