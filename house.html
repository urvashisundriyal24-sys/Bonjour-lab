💬 Useful Sentences

J'habite dans une maison.
I live in a house.

Ma chambre est grande.
My bedroom is big.

La cuisine est petite.
The kitchen is small.

Le canapé est dans le salon.
The sofa is in the living room.

La lampe est sur la table.
The lamp is on the table.

💡 Learning Tip

Learn vocabulary by imagining your own home.

🛏️ La chambre → Your bedroom
🍳 La cuisine → Your kitchen
🛋️ Le salon → Your living room

Try saying the French word every time you see the object!
