✏️ Regular -ER Verbs

Learn the easy pattern behind many French verbs!

✨ What are -ER Verbs?

Many French verbs end in -er.

The good news? Most regular -ER verbs follow the same pattern!

Once you learn the pattern, you can conjugate many verbs.

🧠 The Easy Rule

Take the infinitive verb and remove -er.

parler → parl

Then add the correct ending.

🔢 Three Simple Steps

1️⃣

Find the verb

Example: parler

2️⃣

Remove -er

parler → parl

3️⃣

Add an ending

parl + e = parle

📚 Parler — To Speak

Subject Ending Conjugation English
Je -e je parle I speak
Tu -es tu parles you speak
Il / Elle -e il parle / elle parle he / she speaks
Nous -ons nous parlons we speak
Vous -ez vous parlez you speak
Ils / Elles -ent ils parlent / elles parlent they speak

⭐ Remember the Endings

👤 Je

-e

parle

👉 Tu

-es

parles

👥 Nous

🙋 Vous

🌈 More Regular -ER Verbs

❤️ Aimer

To like / to love

J'aime le café.
I like coffee.

🎵 Chanter

To sing

Je chante.
I sing.

💃 Danser

To dance

Nous dansons.
We dance.

📖 Étudier

To study

J'étudie.
I study.

👂 Écouter

To listen

J'écoute.
I listen.

👀 Regarder

To watch / look at

Je regarde la télévision.
I watch television.

💡 Important Tip

In regular -ER verbs, the endings are usually:

-e • -es • -e • -ons • -ez • -ent

The final -ent of ils / elles is normally silent.

🧠 Memory Trick

👤 Je → E
👉 Tu → ES
👥 Nous → ONS
🙋 Vous → EZ
👨‍👩‍👧 Ils/Elles → ENT
