💡 Useful Expressions

J'ai vingt ans. → I am 20 years old.

J'ai deux frères. → I have two brothers.

J'ai trois livres. → I have three books.

Il y a cinq étudiants. → There are five students.

🌟 Learning Tip

Say each number aloud several times. Then try counting from 0 to 20 without looking at the screen!
